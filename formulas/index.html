Formula gallery

Formulas are a few lines of maths that draw on the display, every pixel at once. Open one to watch it, change its sliders and send it to your display; Remix opens it in the demo's editor. Made one you like? Its page has Submit to the gallery, or read how to add yours.

From the community

Nothing here yet: be the first. Make a formula in the demo, then Copy share link, and on its page, Submit to the gallery.
