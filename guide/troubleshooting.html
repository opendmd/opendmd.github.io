Troubleshooting#

The installer can't find or connect to the board#

  • Use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer, not Firefox, Safari or a phone.
  • Try a different USB cable: many only carry power.
  • On Windows or older macOS, install the driver for the board's USB chip, then plug the board in again: CH340/CH341, CP210x or FTDI. The chip's name is printed on it, next to the USB socket.
  • Hold the board's BOOT button while the installer says "Connecting".

I can't open opendmd.local#

  • Make sure your phone or computer is on the same WiFi as the player.
  • Some devices and networks don't support .local names. Use the player's address instead: find it in your router's list of connected devices, or take the SD card out and power the display on, and the panel shows its address.
  • If the player can't join its saved WiFi (a new router, a changed password), it opens the OpenDMD-Setup network again so you can enter the new details, as on first start.

The panel says there's no card, or the card can't be read#

  • Check the card is pushed fully into its slot.
  • The player needs a FAT32 card. If the web app's Now view says the card needs FAT32, use Format card… there (this erases it), then add your GIFs again. See The SD card.
  • The player keeps checking for a card, so you don’t need to restart it after swapping cards.

The display resets or flickers when it's bright#

The power supply can't keep up at high brightness, especially on mostly white pictures. Lower the brightness, or lower Maximum brightness in Settings → Power if you raised it.

Settings says "Card running at reduced speed" or shows card errors#

The card is wearing out. The player keeps going as best it can, but replace the card soon and copy your GIFs over.

The web app won't load, or looks broken#

The player always has a simple backup page at http://opendmd.local/setup: WiFi, basic playback settings and a restart button. To put the web app right, run the installer again with Erase device unticked.

A WiFi update didn't finish#

If the board's light blinks fast after a firmware update, it is waiting to finish installing from the SD card: make sure the card is in and leave it. If the player still doesn't come back, run the installer again with Erase device unticked.

Starting over#

Running the installer with Erase device ticked wipes the board back to a fresh install: WiFi details, settings and a forgotten security key all go. Your SD card, GIFs and playlists are kept.

Still stuck?#

  • Building the display, the hardware or GIFs: the DMDoS community's Telegram group is where people help each other. The Assembly section of dmdos.net shows each step of the build with a photo.
  • OpenDMD itself: open an issue on GitHub. OpenDMD is an independent project, so please don't ask the DMDoS team to fix it.
