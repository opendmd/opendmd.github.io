What you need#

OpenDMD runs on the DMDoS display: two LED panels, an ESP32 board and a microSD card, built into a 128x32 dot-matrix display. The display is designed by the DMDoS community project, and dmdos.net is where to find everything for it: the parts list with shop links in its Hardware section, step-by-step build instructions with photos in Assembly, and frames in Frame.

This page sums up the parts and what OpenDMD expects of them. Already have a working DMDoS display? Then you have everything: install OpenDMD on it.

1ESP32 DevKit V1 (USB-C)#

The brain of the display: a common ESP32 development board with a USB-C socket. It runs the firmware, drives the panels and serves the web app over WiFi. OpenDMD installs onto it straight from your browser.

Where: the Hardware section of dmdos.net links to it on AliExpress.

2DMDoS Board V3#

The carrier board the ESP32 plugs into, designed and made by MORTACA. It sits on the panels' input connector, holds the microSD card slot and feeds power to the panels. OpenDMD is built for this board's wiring.

Where: mortaca.com, linked from the Hardware section of dmdos.net.

3A microSD card#

Where your GIFs live. DMDoS suggests a 32 GB card, but any card you have will do. OpenDMD reads FAT32 cards; a bigger card that comes as exFAT can be formatted from the web app. See The SD card.

Where: any shop; the Hardware section of dmdos.net has a suggestion.

4Two P4 64x32 LED panels#

Two HUB75 panels with a 4 mm pitch, 256x128 mm each, joined side by side into one 512x128 mm display of 128x32 LEDs.

Where: the Hardware section of dmdos.net links to the panels it uses. Check the size and pitch if you buy elsewhere.

5A USB-C cable and power#

A USB data cable to install OpenDMD from a computer (some cables only carry power), and a USB-C power supply to run the display. The panels draw a lot of current when bright, and a weak supply makes the display reset.

Where: you probably have both already.

6A frame (optional)#

A 3D-printable frame by Janibol of Retromojones is on Thingiverse. It needs a large printer (300x300x400 mm). A printed kit can be ordered from dmdos.net, and @jac72 makes custom wooden frames.

Where: the Frame section of dmdos.net.

Building it#

Follow the Assembly section of dmdos.net: it shows each step with a photo, and takes a few minutes even without any DIY experience. It covers joining the panels, which connector the board goes on, the power leads and the ribbon cable.

You don't need to install the DMDoS firmware before OpenDMD. If the board already runs it, OpenDMD replaces it, and your SD card stays as it is.

GIFs#

OpenDMD comes with no GIFs. The DMDoS GIF pack, DMDos GUI v1 + GIFs, is in the Software section of dmdos.net: a card set up with it plays on OpenDMD as it is. You can also make your own with Create Art, or add any 128x32 GIF from the web app.
