Card speed test

Measures how fast this browser can write files to an SD card. It writes made-up test files (random bytes) into a speedtest folder on the card and reports the timings. Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer.

  1. Put the card in the reader. Use a card you don't mind writing to.
  2. Press Pick the card and choose the card itself (its top level, not a folder on it). Allow editing when the browser asks.
  3. When it finishes, eject the card the normal way and time how long ejecting takes. Type the seconds below.
  4. Press Copy results and paste them back.
