Licences

OpenDMD is open source: MIT or Apache 2.0, at your option.

It is built on other open-source software, used under the licences listed in these notices, with each licence's full text:

  • The firmware: what the installer writes to the board (the firmware, its bootloader and the updater): Espressif's ESP-IDF (Apache 2.0, with FreeRTOS, lwIP, Mbed TLS, wpa_supplicant, FatFs and the C library), the HUB75 display driver, and the Rust crates.
  • The web app: the Rust crates in its WebAssembly, and the headings' font, Silkscreen (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
  • The installer uses ESP Web Tools (Apache 2.0), which your browser loads from jsDelivr.

The player carries the firmware's and the web app's notices too: in the web app, Settings › Device › About › Open-source licences.

The demo's and this site's animations are recordings of OpenDMD's own effects, and the Kenney Pixel Platformer sprites (CC0).
